# E1G2 candidate acquisition summary

**Status:** Pair identity and checksums pass. A fixed-grid comparison with the official LUT supports radiance-scale consistency internally, but does not provide independent validation. Saturation and fill semantics remain unresolved, and the sparse geometry is not full resolution. M2 remains blocked.

**Source data:** PRADAN IIRS product selection generated 2026-09-28. Raw product: `ch2_iir_nri_20260227T1239538758_d_img_d18.zip`. Listed calibrated counterpart: `ch2_iir_nci_20260227T1239538758_d_img_d18.zip`.

**Audit provenance:** Raw ZIP SHA-256 `b29cc17233780a973224f7f6742230235052b4b0f5f08652eefd45f8140ca8f1`; calibrated ZIP SHA-256 `669465771c3b6e092b7da251c5e2c5b5041e57d5578cd25216e4aa818334d5d8`; official archive SHA-256 `5afe64c3efe445e6d0334e8a374756ecab45698494dcee0d19616bf5dcb80720`. No train/validation/test split applies to this single-scene QA. Radiance is reported in the calibrated label's `µW/cm**2/sr/µm`; threshold interpretation is unresolved. The descriptive output figure is `figures/e1g2_radiance_and_footprint.png`. The audit code is committed as `ef6e539c0d1bd6c90f15dedd9b0c83405f291db5`; the earlier project base was `7824126`. The comparison samples every 32nd line and every fifth sample across the valid bands.

**Method:** Downloaded only the selected raw and matching calibrated ZIPs. Both archives passed ZIP CRC validation. Parsed each PDS4 XML label and streamed each embedded QUB to compare its MD5 with the value declared in the label. Then streamed all calibrated and raw QUB planes directly from the ZIP files with `uv run --no-project --with numpy --with matplotlib python scripts/analyze_iirs_e1g2_scene.py`. Counts cover the full cubes. Positive radiance quantiles use every 32nd line and every fifth sample. Read the official E1G2 saturation table, LUT and their XML labels, and inspected the calibrated product's sparse geometry CSV. The LUT entries were applied in band-major order as `raw_count × gain + offset`; sampled LUT radiance in mW was compared with calibrated values divided by 1000. No cube was extracted to disk.

**Observation:** Both labels report start `2026-02-27T12:39:53.8758Z`, stop `2026-02-27T12:50:41.6917Z`, job ID `IIRXXD18CHO2904302NNNN26058141632221_V2_1`, imaging orbit `29042`, dumping orbit `29043`, exposure `e1`, gain `g2`, dimensions `12,210 × 250 × 256`, and band centers spanning 712.3–5009.7 nm. The raw QUB is 1,562,880,000 bytes and matches label MD5 `518420609143e6f119fa50038f22f23f`; the calibrated QUB is 3,125,760,000 bytes and matches label MD5 `b5614f39ec0faa3c136f3dd8c7a13eca`. Calibrated radiance units are labeled `µW/cm**2/sr/µm`. Raw and calibrated ZIP SHA-256 values are recorded in [findings](findings.md).

The scan found 781,440,000 finite calibrated values. On 222 officially valid E1G2 bands, exact counts are 4,829 zero and 42,081 negative band-pixels. Fixed-grid positive median values range from 118.218 to 1,305.46 µW/cm²/sr/µm. The official saturation CSV and its XML also declare µW/cm²/sr/µm, yet 676,834,500 of 677,655,000 values on those valid bands (99.878921%) meet or exceed its per-band saturation-radiance field. If the table values are instead interpreted as mW, only 12 values exceed the converted threshold. Separately, no raw counts on valid bands meet the table's `dynamic_range` field. The LUT comparison gives median calibrated/LUT ratio 1.00375 and median per-band correlation 0.922 over all valid bands. Across valid longwave bands 226–248, the median ratio is 0.995216 and median correlation is 0.899280. This supports internal numerical consistency, not independent calibration validation. Threshold and raw-count comparisons remain conflicting candidate checks, not confirmed saturation labels or masks.

The official sparse geometry CSV has 1,476 records over 246 scan lines; its sampled extent is approximately 5.47°W–3.50°W and 6.53°S–26.86°N. This is low-latitude coverage. It is a coarse grid, not a full-resolution footprint or per-pixel geometry product. The CSV and overview figure are in [metrics](metrics/e1g2_radiance_by_band.csv) and [figures](figures/e1g2_radiance_and_footprint.png).

**Limitations:** Matching labels establish product lineage. The LUT comparison supports internal radiance-scale consistency. It does not provide an independent calibration reference. Threshold semantics, fill and saturation masks, and full-resolution geolocation remain unresolved. The data are Level 1 radiance, not corrected reflectance. No temperature retrieval, thermal correction, or scientific metric was computed. Treat the scan counts as QA observations only.

**Next gate:** Obtain authoritative clarification of the saturation-radiance table's operational meaning and its relation to this product; verify the `dynamic_range` field against raw-detector semantics; identify the cube fill value and a documented per-pixel validity rule. Until then, do not apply the table as a mask or run thermal correction. Then validate radiance scale against an independent reference, full-resolution geometry, and temperature uncertainty against the published E1G2 method and independent references before any 3 µm interpretation.
