# E1G2 candidate acquisition summary

**Status:** Pair identity and checksums pass. A fixed-grid comparison with the official LUT supports radiance-scale consistency internally, but does not provide independent validation. The saturation table XML declares its units, but its operational relation to observed radiance and raw-count values remains unresolved. The cube label gives no fill constant, and geometry is sparse. M2 remains blocked.

**Source data:** PRADAN IIRS product selection generated 2026-09-28. Raw product: `ch2_iir_nri_20260227T1239538758_d_img_d18.zip`. Listed calibrated counterpart: `ch2_iir_nci_20260227T1239538758_d_img_d18.zip`.

**Audit provenance:** Raw ZIP SHA-256 `b29cc17233780a973224f7f6742230235052b4b0f5f08652eefd45f8140ca8f1`; calibrated ZIP SHA-256 `669465771c3b6e092b7da251c5e2c5b5041e57d5578cd25216e4aa818334d5d8`; official archive SHA-256 `5afe64c3efe445e6d0334e8a374756ecab45698494dcee0d19616bf5dcb80720`. No train/validation/test split applies to this single-scene QA. Radiance is reported in the calibrated label's `µW/cm**2/sr/µm`; threshold interpretation is unresolved. The descriptive output figure is `figures/e1g2_radiance_and_footprint.png`. The audit code is committed as `ef6e539c0d1bd6c90f15dedd9b0c83405f291db5`; the earlier project base was `7824126`. The comparison samples every 32nd line and every fifth sample across the valid bands.

**Method:** Downloaded only the selected raw and matching calibrated ZIPs. Both archives passed ZIP CRC validation. Parsed each PDS4 XML label and streamed each embedded QUB to compare its MD5 with the value declared in the label. Then streamed all calibrated and raw QUB planes directly from the ZIP files with `uv run --no-project --with numpy --with matplotlib python scripts/analyze_iirs_e1g2_scene.py`. Counts cover the full cubes. Positive radiance quantiles use every 32nd line and every fifth sample. Read the official E1G2 saturation table, LUT and their XML labels, and inspected the calibrated product's sparse geometry CSV. The LUT entries were applied in band-major order as `raw_count × gain + offset`; sampled LUT radiance in mW was compared with calibrated values divided by 1000. No cube was extracted to disk.

**Observation:** Both labels report start `2026-02-27T12:39:53.8758Z`, stop `2026-02-27T12:50:41.6917Z`, job ID `IIRXXD18CHO2904302NNNN26058141632221_V2_1`, imaging orbit `29042`, dumping orbit `29043`, exposure `e1`, gain `g2`, dimensions `12,210 × 250 × 256`, and band centers spanning 712.3–5009.7 nm. The raw QUB is 1,562,880,000 bytes and matches label MD5 `518420609143e6f119fa50038f22f23f`; the calibrated QUB is 3,125,760,000 bytes and matches label MD5 `b5614f39ec0faa3c136f3dd8c7a13eca`. Calibrated radiance units are labeled `µW/cm**2/sr/µm`. Raw and calibrated ZIP SHA-256 values are recorded in [findings](findings.md).

The scan found 781,440,000 finite calibrated values. On 222 officially valid E1G2 bands, exact counts are 4,829 zero and 42,081 negative band-pixels. Fixed-grid positive median values range from 118.218 to 1,305.46 µW/cm²/sr/µm. The official saturation CSV XML declares µW/cm²/sr/µm, matching the calibrated label. Under that declared unit, 676,834,500 of 677,655,000 values on valid bands (99.878921%) meet or exceed the table's per-band saturation-radiance field. No raw counts on valid bands meet the table's `dynamic_range` field, declared in counts. A counterfactual mW calculation yields 12 radiance exceedances, but the table XML does not support that interpretation. The LUT comparison gives median calibrated/LUT ratio 1.00375 and median per-band correlation 0.922 over all valid bands. Across valid longwave bands 226–248, the median ratio is 0.995216 and median correlation is 0.899280. This supports internal numerical consistency, not independent calibration validation. The threshold and raw-count mismatch remains unexplained; these comparisons are not confirmed saturation labels or masks.

The official geometry label declares 1,476 coordinate records with pixel and line indices. The 250-sample x 12,210-line cube has six geometry samples at each of 246 sampled scan lines spanning lines 0–12,209. This is a sparse control grid, not per-pixel geometry. Its extent is approximately 5.47°W–3.50°W and 6.53°S–26.86°N. This supports low-latitude coverage only. Browse-level registration has a preliminary held-out image-tie diagnostic, but full-resolution geometry remains unvalidated. The per-band CSV and overview figure are in [metrics](metrics/e1g2_radiance_by_band.csv) and [figures](figures/e1g2_radiance_and_footprint.png).

**Limitations:** Matching labels establish product lineage. The LUT comparison supports internal radiance-scale consistency. It does not provide an independent calibration reference. Saturation field application, the fill rule, pixel masks, and full-resolution geolocation remain unresolved. The data are Level 1 radiance, not corrected reflectance. No temperature retrieval, thermal correction, or scientific metric was computed. Treat the scan counts as QA observations only.

**Next gate:** The saturation XML explicitly declares radiance in µW/cm²/sr/µm and dynamic range in counts. Obtain authoritative clarification of how these fields define pixel saturation and why their values conflict with this paired observation. The calibrated cube label has no fill constant, so obtain a documented validity rule before classifying pixel values. Do not apply the table as a mask or run thermal correction. Then validate radiance against an independent reference, register geometry against an independent lunar reference, and quantify temperature uncertainty against the published E1G2 method before any 3 µm interpretation.
