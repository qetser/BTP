- The official mode-validity workbook marks E1G2 bands 226–248 valid, covering 4504.1–4874.9 nm. This is mode-level validity, not a per-pixel quality mask.
- Full-cube audit command: `uv run --no-project --with numpy --with matplotlib python scripts/analyze_iirs_e1g2_scene.py` (script at `scripts/analyze_iirs_e1g2_scene.py`; base revision `7824126`). It streamed all 781,440,000 calibrated values and all matching raw counts band by band from their ZIP archives. Exact counts are full-cube; distribution quantiles are sampled every 32nd line and every fifth sample. No cube was extracted.
- All calibrated values are finite. Across 222 valid E1G2 bands, there are 4,829 exact-zero and 42,081 negative band-pixels. Sampled positive medians range from 118.218 to 1,305.46 µW/cm²/sr/µm. These counts do not classify zero or negative values as fill.
- Both the calibrated product label and official E1G2 saturation-table label declare µW/cm²/sr/µm. Applying the table's direct per-band threshold finds 676,834,500 of 677,655,000 valid-band values (99.878921%) at or above threshold. Treating its values counterfactually as mW yields 12 exceedances. Comparing raw counts with the table's `dynamic_range` column yields zero valid-band exceedances. The source labels do not explain this conflict; none of these candidate comparisons is a confirmed saturation mask.
- The official LUT comparison used `raw_count × gain + offset` in band-major order, consistent with the LUT XML record count and layout. Calibrated values were converted from µW to mW as specified by the SIS. On the fixed sample grid, the median calibrated/LUT ratio is 1.00375 and median per-band correlation is 0.922. For valid bands 226–248, the median ratio is 0.995216 and median correlation is 0.899280. This is an internal consistency check, not independent radiometric validation.
- The calibrated geometry CSV contains 1,476 sparse points over 246 scan lines. Its approximate longitude range is 5.47°W–3.50°W and latitude range is 6.53°S–26.86°N. This confirms the scene is low latitude, but the sparse grid does not provide a full-resolution footprint or per-pixel geometry.

The plotted radiance, LUT ratio, and sparse geometry are in [`e1g2_radiance_and_footprint.png`](figures/e1g2_radiance_and_footprint.png). ## Gate

The raw/calibrated pair passes identity, mode, archive CRC, and declared QUB checksum checks. The official LUT supports internal radiance-scale consistency, but no independent calibration reference was used. The sparse geometry indicates low-latitude coverage. Saturation-table semantics remain unresolved: the threshold as labeled flags nearly all valid calibrated values, while the raw `dynamic_range` field flags none. Do not turn either comparison into a mask. The scene is not ready for M2 until authoritative saturation/fill semantics, independent radiance validation, full-resolution geometry, and correction quality are addressed.
