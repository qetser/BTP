# M1 inventory summary

**Status:** Inventory, pairing, and quality scans are complete. The two E3G2 calibrated products have comparable median magnitudes after applying their label units, but the sampled raw-to-LUT-to-calibrated audit finds a large unresolved magnitude mismatch. Saturation-field application, fill rules, independent radiance validation, and coverage gates remain open.

**Scope:** Two raw/calibrated IIRS pairs observed on 2020-02-08

**Source data:** Four original PRADAN ZIP archives and their extracted PDS4 products in ignored `data/`

**Method:** `python3 scripts/build_iirs_manifest.py --data-root data/pradan --extracted-root data/extracted`

**Evidence:** [`iirs_inventory.csv`](iirs_inventory.csv), [`pilot_lineage.md`](pilot_lineage.md), and [`data_dictionary.md`](data_dictionary.md)

**Validity artifact:** [Mode CSV](metrics/band_validity_by_mode.csv) and [mode figure](figures/band_validity_by_mode.svg). The source is official `iir.zip` SHA-256 `5afe64c3efe445e6d0334e8a374756ecab45698494dcee0d19616bf5dcb80720`, SIS V17 (March 2026), and its band-validity workbook. Wavelength units are nm; validity is a binary flag by band and exposure/gain mode. No train/validation/test split applies to this instrument metadata. Generation code commit: `4ef154b`.

**Pilot radiance quality:** [CSV](metrics/pilot_radiance_quality_by_band.csv) and [figure](figures/pilot_radiance_quality_by_band.png). Both full calibrated cubes were scanned by band. Counts are exact; positive-pixel quantiles use every 32nd line and every 5th sample. The figure compares thresholds under the saturation XML's declared µW unit and a counterfactual mW scenario. The XML unit is known. Field application remains unresolved because the declared thresholds flag nearly all valid-band values. No train/validation/test split applies to this M1 instrument check. Source data are the two calibrated 2020-02-08 cubes and official `iir.zip` above. Code was run from working-tree revision `f58ff4c`; `scripts/analyze_iirs_radiance_pilot.py` was uncommitted at scan time.

**Paired raw–LUT–calibrated E3G2 audit:** [summary](../m1-e3g2-lineage-check/summary.md), [per-band values](../m1-e3g2-lineage-check/metrics/e3g2_raw_lut_calibrated_by_band.csv), and [figure](../m1-e3g2-lineage-check/figures/e3g2_raw_lut_calibrated_diagnostics.png). Both 2020 raw/calibrated pairs were scanned on all 127 E3G2-valid bands. Only one raw count per scene reaches `Dynamic_Range`; the XML-declared µW threshold flags 99.9988% of valid band-pixels, while a counterfactual mW reading flags none. On a fixed sample grid, median per-band archived-Level-1/LUT-stage ratios are about 0.00020, with correlations 0.898 (`m65`) and 0.924 (`d18`). The official guide documents destriping and post-processing after LUT correction. This is an incomplete stage comparison; its magnitude difference is unexplained and does not establish a calibration-scale error. No train/validation/test split applies. Code ran from base commit `304c5e8`; the analysis script is recorded in the module notes.

**Literature and mode-selection record:** The initial targeted review is in [`m1-literature`](../m1-literature/summary.md), with a cited matrix in [`docs/gap_matrix.md`](../../../docs/gap_matrix.md). Published thermal processing and the official validity workbook support E1G2 as the next mode to acquire. They do not resolve the current saturation-unit or per-pixel mask gate.

**Browse figures:** [`m65`](figures/calibrated_browse_m65_four_part_preview.png) and [`d18`](figures/calibrated_browse_d18_four_part_preview.png). They use the calibrated-product browse PNGs (PDS4 label version 1.0), not the radiance cube values. The browse images are 8-bit display products with no physical radiance unit. No train/validation/test split applies to these M1 inventory previews. The generation command is `uv run --no-project --with pillow python scripts/build_iirs_strip_previews.py`; code commit `0dd800d`.

All four extracted cubes match their PDS4 MD5 and declared byte size. Each raw product has an exact calibrated match by observation time, job ID, orbit, dimensions, and wavelength table. The calibrated labels explicitly call their cubes radiance spectral images, but declare different units for the two pairs. Neither product is validated reflectance.

The supplied official archive confirms that the calibrated cubes are Level 1 radiance. On officially valid `E3G2` bands, sampled positive-pixel medians in the two cubes have comparable magnitudes after converting each declared unit to mW/cm²/sr/µm. This describes the labels but does not validate their numerical accuracy. A separate paired LUT-stage/Level-1 audit finds that the values track variation, with median ratios near 0.0002. Appendix F documents destriping and radiance post-processing after LUT correction; the local archive has no destriping coefficients, so the magnitude difference remains unexplained. Zero values are rare and no nonfinite values occur on valid bands; zero is not confirmed as fill. The saturation-table XML declares units, but applying those values flags nearly all valid-band radiance samples under that declared unit; the alternative mW comparison flags none. Do not use either comparison as a saturation mask. Both E3G2 pilots' valid bands stop at 3155.9 nm, so thermal correction requiring 4–5 µm and all 3 µm reflectance work remain blocked. The two current tracks do not meet the independent coverage gate for later evaluation. The 2026 E1G2 audit and current saturation/fill clarification are recorded in [the E1G2 module](../m1-e1g2-scene/summary.md).

The initial four raw scenes are retained only as a historical manifest: [`iirs_inventory_initial_four_scenes.csv`](iirs_inventory_initial_four_scenes.csv). The validity CSV and figure document instrument metadata; no reflectance or scientific performance metric has been computed.
