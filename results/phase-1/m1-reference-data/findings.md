# M1 reference-data findings

- The E1G2 footprint at approximately 5.47°W–3.50°W and 6.53°S–26.86°N is a low-latitude strip. LROC lists a global WAC morphologic mosaic at 100 m/pixel, which is a candidate spatial reference. Its ground sampling is comparable in scale to IIRS, but mosaicking, illumination, and sensor differences require independent control-point review.
- The PDS M³ Level-2 archive describes products as pixel-located and reflectance-calibrated. The IIRS Level-2 literature also describes same-region M³ comparisons. This supports a future cross-instrument reflectance check after IIRS corrections, not a direct check of current Level-1 radiance.
- The public PDS ODE REST service's M³ `REFIMG` footprint-intersection query returned five Level-2 candidates for the E1G2 bounding box: IDs `M3G20090205T211213_V01_L2`, `M3G20090205T225833_V01_L2`, `M3G20090205T233034_V01_L2`, `M3G20090206T010833_V01_L2`, and `M3G20090206T030351_V01_L2`, all orbit 1080. Their metadata are saved in [`m3_overlap_candidates.csv`](m3_overlap_candidates.csv) and generated by [`query_m3_overlap.py`](../../../scripts/query_m3_overlap.py).
- ODE's footprint-intersection result is a candidate selection only. The M³ labels and usable pixel masks still need review to establish actual common valid coverage, and no cube has been downloaded.
- The current workspace has no acquired WAC or M³ reference files. No overlap area, registration residual, reflectance error, or pass/fail result has been computed.
- Chauhan et al. (2025) use Apollo 16 ground observations for calibration and same-region M³ for comparison. Apollo 16 is outside the current E1G2 footprint and cannot serve as this scene's local ground target.
- A recent preprint reports SIFT registration of IIRS strips to LRO WAC, but it is not peer reviewed. It is a candidate method to evaluate, not validation evidence for this scene. Prefer held-out control points and report residuals rather than adopting the paper's performance claim.
