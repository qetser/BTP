Retrieved ODE's geographic footprints in [`m3_ode_footprints.json`](m3_ode_footprints.json). Counted the 1,476 sparse IIRS geometry-table coordinates lying inside each M³ footprint using [`analyze_m3_control_grid_overlap.py`](../../../scripts/analyze_m3_control_grid_overlap.py).

**Observations:** `M3G20090206T010833_V01_L2` contains 1,033 of 1,476 IIRS geometry control points (69.99%) inside its ODE-reported footprint. The next candidate contains 703 (47.63%); the other three contain 259, 5, and 0. This ranks footprints for inspection only. The IIRS geometry grid is sparse and M³ per-pixel validity has not been checked. All five M³ labels describe 304-sample, 85-band Level-2 reflectance cubes, flag thermal correction as applied, declare -999.0 as a special pixel, and define stored 1.0 as 100% reflectance. A WAC crop is now stored locally at `data/reference/lroc_wac_e1g2/e1g2_wac_crop.pgm` (ignored by Git). The acquisition read 164 source TIFF tiles by HTTP byte range, transferring 10,747,904 raster bytes rather than the 1,617,959,634-byte global pyramid. The crop is 824 x 10,230 8-bit pixels at 100 m/pixel, with a 20 km margin around projected E1G2 control coordinates. Retrieval and projection metadata are in [`wac_crop_metadata.json`](wac_crop_metadata.json); the acquisition is reproducible with [`fetch_wac_reference_crop.py`](../../../scripts/fetch_wac_reference_crop.py). The crop is not yet registered to IIRS.

**Limitations:** Control-point counts are not pixel-area or valid-pixel overlap fractions and cannot establish M³ coverage quality. No train/validation/test split applies to this footprint-screening diagnostic. WAC image matching can assess spatial registration but cannot establish IIRS radiometric calibration. M³ comparison applies to reflectance products, not the current IIRS radiance cube. Spectral response, pixel footprint, acquisition geometry, and residual registration differences must be handled before comparing spectra.

## Preliminary WAC-to-IIRS browse registration

**Method:** Interpolated the sparse IIRS longitude/latitude grid across the browse image, projected each location into the WAC orthographic GeoTIFF coordinates, and warped WAC into the IIRS browse grid. Matched local x/y image gradients in 83 100 x 192 browse-pixel windows using CLAHE and normalized cross-correlation. Accepted 23 training tie windows and 8 held-out tie windows (NCC >= 0.15), with held-out windows in two separated 1,000-line spatial blocks. A robust linear along-track correction was fit only on the training ties; residuals were computed on held-out ties. Exact commands and software versions are recorded in [`metrics/iirs_wac_registration_browse_summary.json`](metrics/iirs_wac_registration_browse_summary.json).

**Observation:** Held-out residual RMSE is 0.781 IIRS cube-pixel equivalents (57.4 m in WAC projected map units); the 95th percentile is 1.075 cube-pixel equivalents (78.0 projected metres). The original browse is 175 x 8,547 pixels for a 250-sample x 12,210-line cube, so IIRS-pixel equivalents multiply browse offsets by the 1.42857 browse-to-cube scale. Projected metres use the local Jacobian from IIRS pixels through the interpolated geolocation grid into the WAC map; these are not lunar surface ground distances. The figure plots training/held-out offsets and residuals: [`figures/iirs_wac_registration_browse_residuals.svg`](figures/iirs_wac_registration_browse_residuals.svg). Per-window source values are in [`metrics/iirs_wac_registration_browse_ties.csv`](metrics/iirs_wac_registration_browse_ties.csv).

**Split:** Along-track spatial blocks, every fourth 1,000-line block held out, with guard zones 1.5 patch heights wide. Eight accepted held-out image-tie windows span two blocks. These are spatial image patches, not independent ground-survey control points.

**Data version:** IIRS browse `ch2_iir_nci_20260227T1239538758_b_brw_d18.png`, created `2026-03-13T20:29:24`, MD5 `8f8c9caabf07e346bd2afa38383c34a4`; WAC pyramid `WAC_GLOBAL_O000N0000_100M.PYR.TIF`, last-modified `2026-08-14T18:41:05Z`, ETag `"e2137517b2bb1afa7232c407ebbd1ada"`. Split is spatial, not a train/validation/test data-set split.

**Limitations and gate:** This is a provisional browse-scale consistency diagnostic. The browse image is lower resolution than the IIRS cube, only eight accepted held-out patches were available, and the held-out patches occupy two regions of the same observation. The residual is relative image alignment after a trained correction, not surveyed absolute ground-control error. It does not establish full-resolution geometry or radiometric calibration. Keep the geometry gate open until the transform is checked against a full-resolution IIRS band and additional spatially separated held-out ties; seek independent absolute controls if available.

## Full-resolution WAC-to-IIRS band-40 diagnostic

**Method:** Extracted calibrated E1G2 band 40 (1,369.6 nm), listed as valid by the official E1G2 band-validity table. The extractor streams to the selected BSQ band, stretches finite values between the 1st and 99th percentiles for display, and saves the PNG under ignored `data/reference/`. Used the same geometry-based initial WAC warp, gradient-patch matcher, along-track offset model, spatial-block split, and guard zones as the browse comparison.

**Observation:** 30 accepted training ties and 12 held-out ties across 3 spatial blocks. Held-out RMSE is 2.757 IIRS pixels (198.2 WAC projected map metres); the 95th percentile is 5.317 pixels (382.7 projected map metres). These are larger than the browse-scale residuals. This may reflect tie-match ambiguity and finer spatial structure; no absolute accuracy conclusion follows from the residuals. The source values and figure are [`metrics/iirs_wac_registration_band040_1369nm_ties.csv`](metrics/iirs_wac_registration_band040_1369nm_ties.csv), [`metrics/iirs_wac_registration_band040_1369nm_summary.json`](metrics/iirs_wac_registration_band040_1369nm_summary.json), and [`figures/iirs_wac_registration_band040_1369nm_residuals.svg`](figures/iirs_wac_registration_band040_1369nm_residuals.svg).

**Split:** Spatial along-track blocks of 1,000 image lines, every fourth block held out with a 1.5 patch-height guard. The 12 accepted held-out image-tie windows span three blocks. This is a spatial holdout for tie patches, not an orbit-level train/validation/test split.

**Data version and units:** IIRS calibrated image archive `ch2_iir_nci_20260227T1239538758_d_img_d18.zip`, band 40 at 1,369.6 nm. The display PNG MD5 and percentile stretch are in the JSON metric metadata. WAC pyramid last-modified `2026-08-14T18:41:05Z`, ETag `"e2137517b2bb1afa7232c407ebbd1ada"`. Residuals are in IIRS cube pixels and WAC projected map metres calculated from the local IIRS-to-WAC coordinate Jacobian. Projected map metres are not lunar ground distances.

**Code and reproduction:** `scripts/extract_iirs_registration_band.py` and `scripts/register_iirs_to_wac.py` in the current work unit (record the commit after commit). Extract with `uv run --python 3.11 --with numpy --with opencv-python-headless python scripts/extract_iirs_registration_band.py`; register with the full-resolution command in [`notes.md`](notes.md). Software versions are recorded in the JSON metric metadata.

**Limitations and gate:** This uses one display-stretched radiance band only to match surface morphology. Its validity designation does not validate radiance scale or pixel masks. Image ties are not surveyed ground controls. Twelve accepted held-out patches across three parts of one observation and an RMSE of 2.757 pixels do not close the geolocation gate. Review low-confidence matches, add well-separated control regions, and seek absolute controls before treating geometry as validated.
