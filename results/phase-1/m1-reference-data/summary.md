# M1 independent reference-data plan

**Status:** Five M³ Level-2 candidates identified and label-reviewed. A sparse control-grid screen ranks `M3G20090206T010833_V01_L2` first for follow-up. A footprint-sized LROC WAC crop has been acquired for geometry registration. No M³ image cube or valid-pixel array was downloaded, and no registration residual or spectral validation has been computed.

**Source data:** Candidate IIRS scene `20260227T1239538758` (E1G2), with local footprint of about 5.47°W–3.50°W and 6.53°S–26.86°N. Its geometry file contains 1,476 sparse coordinate records across 246 of 250 lines.

**Method:** Queried the PDS Orbital Data Explorer REST service for M³ `REFIMG` products using `loc=f` (footprint intersection) and the E1G2 bounds (-7° to 27° latitude, 354.5° to 356.5° east longitude). Reviewed all five PDS3 labels and recorded their IDs, observation times, dimensions, band count, thermal-correction flag, special-pixel value, and reflectance scale in [`m3_label_review.csv`](m3_label_review.csv). Retrieved ODE's geographic footprints in [`m3_ode_footprints.json`](m3_ode_footprints.json). Counted the 1,476 sparse IIRS geometry-table coordinates lying inside each M³ footprint using [`analyze_m3_control_grid_overlap.py`](../../../scripts/analyze_m3_control_grid_overlap.py).

**Observations:** `M3G20090206T010833_V01_L2` contains 1,033 of 1,476 IIRS geometry control points (69.99%) inside its ODE-reported footprint. The next candidate contains 703 (47.63%); the other three contain 259, 5, and 0. This ranks footprints for inspection only. The IIRS geometry grid is sparse and M³ per-pixel validity has not been checked. All five M³ labels describe 304-sample, 85-band Level-2 reflectance cubes, flag thermal correction as applied, declare -999.0 as a special pixel, and define stored 1.0 as 100% reflectance. A WAC crop is now stored locally at `data/reference/lroc_wac_e1g2/e1g2_wac_crop.pgm` (ignored by Git). The acquisition read 164 source TIFF tiles by HTTP byte range, transferring 10,747,904 raster bytes rather than the 1,617,959,634-byte global pyramid. The crop is 824 x 10,230 8-bit pixels at 100 m/pixel, with a 20 km margin around projected E1G2 control coordinates. Retrieval and projection metadata are in [`wac_crop_metadata.json`](wac_crop_metadata.json); the acquisition is reproducible with [`fetch_wac_reference_crop.py`](../../../scripts/fetch_wac_reference_crop.py). The crop is not yet registered to IIRS.

**Limitations:** Control-point counts are not pixel-area or valid-pixel overlap fractions and cannot establish M³ coverage quality. No train/validation/test split applies to this footprint-screening diagnostic. WAC image matching can assess spatial registration but cannot establish IIRS radiometric calibration. M³ comparison applies to reflectance products, not the current IIRS radiance cube. Spectral response, pixel footprint, acquisition geometry, and residual registration differences must be handled before comparing spectra.
