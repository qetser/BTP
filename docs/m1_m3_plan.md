# M1–M3 implementation plan

**Status:** planning, based on the local archive inventory and the primary sources in the [README](../README.md). This plan covers a first defensible experimental release, not the later foundation-model stages.

## Objective and decision gate

Produce an auditable IIRS reflectance corpus, a limited but credible label set, and baseline results on held-out orbital tracks/provinces. Begin M4 only when (1) raw-to-reflectance quality is demonstrated, (2) test observations are geographically independent of training, and (3) at least one physical and one learned baseline can be evaluated without label or preprocessing leakage.

## M1 — acquisition, inventory, and gap analysis

| Step | Work | Output / pass criterion |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Product inventory | Parse every ZIP's PDS4 XML; capture product ID, processing level, dimensions, data type, wavelengths and widths, byte size, checksum, footprint, solar incidence, acquisition time, and browse image. Verify the extracted `.qub` byte count and checksum. | `manifests/iirs_inventory.csv` and an exception log. No silent missing fields or checksum failures. |
| 2. Documentation and product pair | Download ISRO's `iir.zip` product guide/SIS from PRADAN and identify the corresponding **calibrated** product for each raw scene. Determine whether that product is radiance or a higher-level reflectance product from its label and guide, not its filename. | One-page data dictionary with units, fill/saturation values, calibration level, and exact raw→calibrated lineage. |
| 3. Sampling design | Add IIRS scenes across multiple nonadjacent tracks, mare/highland provinces, illumination conditions, acquisition times, and, if the later polar question remains in scope, polar coverage. Use footprint maps to prevent overlap across splits. | Scene-selection table and fixed `train/val/test` track/province assignment. The three-scene, low-latitude pilot does not satisfy this gate. |
| 4. Comparators and labels | Acquire overlapping [M³ L2 reflectance](https://pds.nasa.gov/ds-view/pds/viewProfile.jsp?dsid=CH1-ORB-L-M3-4-L2-REFLECTANCE-V1.0), [USGS geologic-unit polygons](https://astrogeology.usgs.gov/search/map/Moon/Geology/Unified_Geologic_Map_of_the_Moon_GIS_v2/), and suitable mineral spectra from [USGS Spectral Library v7](https://www.usgs.gov/labs/spectroscopy-lab/usgs-spectral-library). Register coordinate systems and wavelength response before comparison. | Provenance manifest for every reference and an overlap/quality report. Polygon labels are tagged as weak labels. |
| 5. Gap matrix | Record for each target task the available IIRS scenes, comparable M³ coverage, label source, coverage and resolution mismatch, and related method/paper. Verify novelty claims separately before publication; this initial plan does not establish a literature-wide absence claim. | `docs/gap_matrix.md` with citations, known limitations, and unresolved acquisition gaps. |

**Practical order:** run steps 1–2 on the existing files first; expand the scene collection only after the XML/guide interpretation is sound. The supplied IIRS User Guide V6 documents naming, apparent-reflectance conversion, and post-LUT processing. It says calibrated values use mW, while the matched E1G2 label says µW; the numeric unit remains unresolved. The LUT comparison is near unity only after applying the label's unit conversion and is not independent validation. The matched pair passes label identity and archive/cube checksum checks; see the [acquisition record](../results/phase-1/m1-e1g2-scene/summary.md) and [gate follow-up](../results/phase-1/m1-gate-followup-20261010/summary.md). A PDS ODE query returned five M³ Level-2 candidates. The sparse geometry-point screen ranks `M3G20090206T010833_V01_L2` first, with 69.99% of IIRS geometry grid points inside its footprint; valid pixel coverage remains unknown. A crop of the LROC WAC reference has been acquired. Browse-scale ties reported 0.781 cube-pixel-equivalent held-out RMSE; the full-resolution band-40 comparison reported 2.757 IIRS-pixel RMSE with 12 held-out ties across three blocks. Both are relative image-match diagnostics, not absolute geolocation validation. Review match confidence and acquire well-distributed controls before accepting geometry. See the [M1 reference-data record](../results/phase-1/m1-reference-data/summary.md). Document PRADAN access details without putting credentials or session files in the repo. Retain original archives as immutable inputs.

## M2 — calibration, correction, and analysis-ready patches

1. **Decode and verify.** Read PDS4 axes and `UnsignedLSB2` type, compare `.qub` size/MD5 with its XML, and inspect per-band histograms, line profiles, browse orientation, fill values, saturation, dropped lines, striping, and spatial registration. Use chunked I/O. Reject corrupt products before correction.
2. **Establish the radiance source.** Prefer an official calibrated IIRS product as the radiance starting point. If reproducing counts-to-radiance from raw, implement only from the ISRO guide/calibration files and verify against an official calibrated counterpart. A raw-count cube must not be relabeled as radiance.
3. **Geometry and masks.** Use the supplied geometry products or validated orbital geometry to obtain per-pixel location and illumination/viewing angles. Build explicit per-pixel/per-band masks for invalid, saturated, low-signal, and poorly calibrated measurements. Keep both the source and reason for each mask.
4. **Reflectance and thermal component.** Convert calibrated radiance to apparent reflectance using wavelength-matched solar irradiance and incidence geometry. For the long-wavelength region, estimate and subtract thermal radiance using the published IIRS method before computing 3 µm reflectance. Compare temperature estimates and residual spectra against source-paper examples and/or an official L2 scene. Preserve pre/post correction spectra.
5. **Lunar photometry.** Apply the validated lunar photometric model from the IIRS literature, with documented reference geometry and error bounds. Do not use terrestrial atmospheric correction. Cross-check overlapping M³ spectral shapes only after accounting for resolution, acquisition geometry, and wavelength-response differences.
6. **Patch export.** Create reflectance tiles with wavelength centers/widths, valid-band mask, spatial footprint, acquisition/orbit ID, correction version, split ID, and uncertainty/quality flags. Compute normalization statistics on training tracks only.

**M2 quality gate:** confirm numerical units and mode-specific band validity; check sample spectra against expected broad absorption behavior; map residual striping; quantify invalid-pixel fractions by band and scene; compare overlapping M³/official L2 spectra with plots and summary errors. A 3 µm band-depth product is blocked until the thermal-correction residual and signal-to-noise checks pass. The current `E3G2` pilot has no valid 4–5 µm bands. Verma et al. (2022) used `E1G2` radiance data and the 2.5–5 µm range for thermal correction. SIS Appendix H explicitly says to treat only `VALID` bands as valid; `SATURATION`, `OSF`, and `INVALID` are excluded as bands. It marks E1G2 bands 226–248 (4504.1–4874.9 nm) valid, and a matched E1G2 pair has been acquired. This is band-level validity, not a per-pixel mask. Its full-cube scan finds 99.878921% of values on officially valid bands at or above the saturation table's threshold under its declared µW/cm²/sr/µm unit, while no paired raw count reaches the table's `Dynamic_Range` field, declared in counts. The calibrated QUB label declares no missing-value constant. The V6 guide says calibrated products use mW, while the E1G2 label declares µW. Its LUT comparison gives a median ratio of 1.00375 only after applying the label's unit conversion and is not independent calibration validation. Keep this conflict unresolved and do not assign masks from the threshold mismatch. Chowdhury et al. (2020) describe laboratory count-to-radiance characterization, including saturation radiance. A 2025 LPSC abstract says a separate recalibration uses the ISRO LUT, saturation radiance, and CCD dynamic range together to convert linearized Level-0 counts to radiance. That study reports differences from the ISRO Level-1 product and does not define Level-1 pixel masks or fill values. This is a method comparator, not authority for interpreting the local mismatch. Published alternatives are now recorded: IIRS/M³ unity-normalized comparison checks spectral shape only; the Apollo 16/RELAB 62231 method supports an absolute-reflectance check on another IIRS scene; and Diviner comparison matched by ground control points and acquisition time can check derived temperature under the published conditions, not IIRS radiance units. These checks do not resolve local Level-1 semantics. The 2026 sensitivity analysis shows that temperature uncertainty can materially affect reflectance near 3.16 µm. See the [initial gap matrix](gap_matrix.md) and [M1 gate follow-up](../results/phase-1/m1-gate-followup-20261010/summary.md). Learned denoising is an optional controlled ablation after this gate, never a substitute for physical calibration.

**Small-sample and cross-mode table check (2026-10-03):** A separate fixed-line probe used 16 scan lines across the E1G2 strip, all 250 samples, and all 222 valid bands (888,000 band-pixel observations). It found 97.8048% of radiances at or above the saturation field and zero raw counts at or above `Dynamic_Range`. Per-band median LUT ratios range from 0.9060 to 1.0960 despite a median of 1.00139. Applying each official LUT at `Dynamic_Range` gives median radiance/saturation-number ratios of 1.559 for E1G2 and 5,118 for E3G2 if saturation values are treated counterfactually as mW. Both XML labels declare µW, and the large mode difference does not support a single unit correction. See the [sample and table-check record](../results/phase-1/m1-saturation-sample/summary.md). Do not create a mask from these diagnostic comparisons.

**Full-resolution relative registration candidate rule (2026-10-08):** For image ties against a lunar morphology reference (WAC or an illumination-matched DEM hillshade), require held-out median residual <= 1.0 IIRS pixel, p95 <= 2.0 pixels, at least three cross-track columns, at least four spatial holdout blocks, and no approximate 10 km line bin with at least three ties having median residual > 1.5 pixels. Select match-score cutoffs and reference-rendering hypotheses from training ties only; report held-out results without tuning on them. This rule tests relative image consistency; it does not establish absolute geolocation accuracy. The first WAC 2D grid run failed. A later SLDEM2015 hillshade run excluded nodata-intersecting patches and passed all five candidate criteria with 34 held-out ties, median residual 0.375 px, p95 1.487 px, and RMSE 0.699 px. Its training-fitted offset field has been applied to a separate sparse-geometry candidate and its held-out residuals reproduced. See the [WAC grid record](../results/phase-1/m1-wac-2d-grid/summary.md), [SLDEM hillshade record](../results/phase-1/m1-sldem-hillshade/summary.md), and [candidate correction record](../results/phase-1/m1-sldem-correction-candidate/summary.md). Keep absolute geometry provisional until the candidate is evaluated against independent controls or an independently corrected product.

**E3G2 paired LUT audit (2026-10-03; guide review updated 2026-10-10):** Both matched 2020 pairs were compared on all 127 valid bands. Only one raw count per scene meets `Dynamic_Range`. The XML-declared threshold flags 99.9988% of valid-band pixels; a counterfactual mW threshold flags none. After applying each cube's PDS4 unit, median per-band Level-1/LUT-stage ratios are about 0.0002; median per-band correlations are 0.898 (`m65`) and 0.924 (`d18`). The V6 user guide says destriping and radiance post-processing follow LUT correction and explicitly attributes differences from direct raw/LUT calculations to these steps. This provides a general explanation for the stage difference, but does not quantify the observed ratio or reproduce these products. Separately, the guide states calibrated radiance units as mW while the matched E1G2 label states µW; this conflict remains unresolved. Published alternative validation paths and their limits are recorded in the [M1 gate follow-up](../results/phase-1/m1-gate-followup-20261010/summary.md). Do not assign masks or continue to M2 from this comparison alone.

## M3 — baselines and evaluation

| Baseline | Input and method | Valid comparison |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Mineral spectral parameters | Continuum removal and diagnostic 1 and 2 µm band position/depth, plus spectral angle or matched spectra against wavelength-convolved mineral references. | Qualitative spectral maps; macro-F1/balanced accuracy only on reviewed class labels. Do not equate a geologic map unit with a pure mineral. |
| Hydration index | Continuum-interpolated 3 µm absorption depth on **thermally corrected, sufficiently illuminated** pixels, with propagated mask and uncertainty. | Compare spectral profiles and spatial patterns with M³ where matched; report no water-ice accuracy without independent ice labels. |
| Unsupervised clustering | PCA or other fixed feature reduction followed by k-means, with `k` selected on training/validation scenes; assess stability across seeds and tracks. | Cluster maps and stability. If class scores are shown, align clusters to classes using training labels only, then score on test. |
| From-scratch learned model | A small spectral-spatial CNN trained from random weights, with spatially blocked patch sampling, class weighting, and documented hyperparameters/seeds. | Same reviewed labels, test tracks, masks, and metrics as the supervised classical baseline. Report mean and spread over several seeds. |

**Evaluation protocol:** freeze the orbit/province split before label cleaning or model tuning. Exclude geologic-unit boundaries at a margin appropriate to the coarse map scale, review representative spectra, and keep uncertain labels separate. Report class support, confusion matrix, macro-F1, balanced accuracy, and per-class IoU for actual segmentation labels. Bootstrap uncertainty by **track or region**, not by individual pixel. Include scene-level failure examples and correction/label sensitivity analyses. A model result is only comparable to another when both use the same corrected input version, split, label version, and valid-pixel mask.

## Suggested sequence and milestones

| Window | Milestone | Exit condition |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Weeks 1–2 | M1 inventory, guide, calibrated counterparts, sampling design | Verified manifest and fixed acquisition list |
| Weeks 3–5 | M2 calibrated reflectance pipeline on one pilot strip, then all selected strips | Quality report and reproducible patch dataset |
| Weeks 6–7 | M3 physical baselines, labels, clustering, from-scratch CNN | Held-out scoreboard with class support and uncertainty |
| Week 8 | Reproduce results from clean inputs, write limitations and M4 handoff | Saved configurations, manifests, figures, and pass/fail gate record |

These are planning estimates; PRADAN availability, calibrated-product access, and label quality may change the order or duration.

## Open decisions to resolve from evidence

- **Polar extension:** locate suitable polar IIRS strips and measure whether indirectly illuminated shadowed pixels have enough signal for 3 µm spectroscopy. If not, limit IIRS hydration mapping to sunlit terrain and keep polar ice inference as a separate multi-instrument question. [M³ polar study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6130389/).
- **Calibration authority:** the SIS and local labels establish the active calibrated products as Level 1 radiance. A 2025 paper describes an IIRS Level-2 reflectance method, but public availability of scene-matched derived products and the operational rules for saturation and fill values remain unverified. The E1G2 saturation XML specifies units, but applying its values produces an unresolved mismatch with the paired cubes. Check PRADAN for any matching Level-2 product and authoritative mask guidance. [PRADAN FAQ](https://pradan.issdc.gov.in/ch2/faq.xhtml); [IIRS L2 processing paper](https://doi.org/10.1111/maps.70037).
- **Ground truth:** choose a small reviewed mineral-class task that the available references can support. Treat the [USGS 1:5M map](https://astrogeology.usgs.gov/search/map/Moon/Geology/Unified_Geologic_Map_of_the_Moon_GIS_v2/) as geologic context and weak labels, not a per-pixel abundance map.
